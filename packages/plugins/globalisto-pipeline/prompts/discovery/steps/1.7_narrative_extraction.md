# 1.7: NARRATIVE EXTRACTION

## PURPOSE

Extract character/human material from hotness search into structured narrative_cast.

This step answers: "Who specifically can we tell this story through?"

---

## INPUT

| Input | Source |
|-------|--------|
| `hotness_results` | From 1.6 |
| `narrative_decomposition` | From 1.5 |

---

## OUTPUT

```yaml
narrative_cast:  # REQUIRED - Discovery fails if empty
  - role_id: NR_01
    role: "[role name]"
    specificity: "[from decomposition]"
    found_individuals:
      - name: "[full name]"
        description: "[who they are, 1 sentence]"
        actions: ["[specific action 1]", "[specific action 2]"]
        quotable: "[direct quote if found]"
        source: "[url]"
    discourse_quality: HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW

  - role_id: NR_02
    # ...
```

---

## PROCESS

### Step 1: Extract Named Individuals

From each hotness_result, extract people who:

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Named | Full name or verifiable identity |
| Acted | Took specific, describable actions |
| Connected | Actions relate to the topic/belief |
| Quotable | Said something on record OR actions documented |

### Step 2: Validate Each Individual

For each extracted individual:

```
□ Has full name (not "sources say", "a former employee")
□ Has specific action (not "was involved in")
□ Connects to the belief/topic
□ Is quotable or documentable
```

**INVALID examples:**
- "Sources familiar with the matter" → no name
- "Critics say" → no specific person
- "A former consultant" → not identifiable
- "Victims of the opioid crisis" → no individual

**VALID examples:**
- "Ryan Hampton, recovery advocate who testified before Congress"
- "Martin Elling, McKinsey partner who pleaded guilty to obstruction"
- "Eric Eyre, Charleston Gazette reporter who won Pulitzer for opioid coverage"

### Step 3: Assess Discourse Quality

For each role:

| Quality | Criteria |
|---------|----------|
| HIGH | Named individuals found, quotable, multiple sources |
| MEDIUM | Named individuals found, limited quotes |
| LOW | Generic discourse only, no specific individuals |

---

## NARRATIVE_CAST REQUIREMENTS

**Discovery FAILS gate if:**
- narrative_cast is empty
- All roles have LOW discourse_quality
- Zero named individuals across all roles

**Discovery PASSES if:**
- At least 2 roles have found_individuals
- At least 1 named individual is quotable
- At least 1 role has HIGH or MEDIUM quality

---

## GATE: NARRATIVE SUFFICIENCY

After this step, use DeepSeek as adversary:

```
Prompt: "Given this narrative_cast, name the specific people
         and what they did. Be concrete."

PASS: Can name people + actions
      "Ryan Hampton testified about losing friends to opioids"
      "Martin Elling emailed about deleting documents"

FAIL: Can only generalize
      "victims were affected by the crisis"
      "insiders had concerns"
```

---

## WORKED EXAMPLE

**Input:** Hotness results from McKinsey opioid search

**Output:**

```yaml
narrative_cast:
  - role_id: NR_01
    role: "victims"
    specificity: "families who testified at hearings"
    found_individuals:
      - name: "Ryan Hampton"
        description: "Recovery advocate who lost friends to opioid addiction"
        actions:
          - "Testified at congressional hearing about personal loss"
          - "Publicly called out McKinsey's role in the crisis"
        quotable: "They put a dollar value on human life"
        source: "https://..."
    discourse_quality: HIGH

  - role_id: NR_02
    role: "discoverer"
    specificity: "journalists who broke the story"
    found_individuals:
      - name: "Michael Forsythe"
        description: "New York Times reporter on McKinsey opioid documents"
        actions:
          - "Obtained court documents showing $14,810 figure"
          - "Co-authored investigation published February 2019"
        quotable: null  # No direct quote found
        source: "https://nytimes.com/..."
    discourse_quality: MEDIUM

  - role_id: NR_03
    role: "insider"
    specificity: "former McKinsey consultants"
    found_individuals: []  # None found with names
    discourse_quality: LOW

  - role_id: NR_04
    role: "enforcer"
    specificity: "prosecutors"
    found_individuals:
      - name: "Joshua Levy"
        description: "Acting US Attorney for Massachusetts"
        actions:
          - "Announced criminal charges against McKinsey"
          - "Stated 'first time a consulting firm held criminally responsible'"
        quotable: "This resolution marks the first time..."
        source: "DOJ press release"
    discourse_quality: HIGH
```

**Gate result:** PASS (3 roles have individuals, 2 are quotable)

---

## HARD CONSTRAINTS

- narrative_cast is REQUIRED output (not optional)
- Empty narrative_cast = Discovery FAIL
- Each individual must be named and validated
- Discourse quality must be assessed per role

---

## COMPLETION RULE

Done when:
- All roles processed
- Named individuals extracted where found
- Discourse quality assessed
- narrative_cast structured and validated
- Adversary gate executed
