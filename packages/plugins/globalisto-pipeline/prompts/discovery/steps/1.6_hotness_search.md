# 1.6: HOTNESS SEARCH

## PURPOSE

Search WHERE discourse is hot for each role identified in decomposition.

This step answers: "Where are people talking about this, and what are they saying?"

---

## INPUT

| Input | Source |
|-------|--------|
| `narrative_decomposition` | From 1.5 |
| `topic` | Module input |

---

## OUTPUT

```yaml
hotness_results:
  - role_id: NR_01
    search_results:
      - source_type: "[reddit | forum | testimony | journalism | etc.]"
        url: "[url]"
        hotness_signal: "[why this is hot: engagement, emotion, specificity]"
        relevant_content: "[excerpt or summary]"
        named_individuals: ["[names found]"]
```

---

## PROCESS

### Step 1: Execute Searches

For each role in `narrative_decomposition`:

1. Use the `search_vectors` provided
2. Search the `discourse_locations` identified
3. Prioritize HIGH ENGAGEMENT sources

### Step 2: Evaluate Hotness

**Hot discourse has:**

| Signal | What to Look For |
|--------|------------------|
| Engagement | High comments, shares, replies |
| Emotion | Strong reactions, personal stakes |
| Specificity | Named people, specific incidents |
| First-person | "I was there", "my family", "I worked at" |

**Not hot (skip):**

| Skip | Why |
|------|-----|
| News aggregation | Just repeating facts |
| Memes/jokes | Not substantive |
| Speculation without sources | "I heard that..." |
| Generic commentary | No specific knowledge |

### Step 3: Extract Named Individuals

For each hot source, extract:
- Names mentioned (full names preferred)
- What they did or said
- Their relationship to the topic

---

## SOURCE PRIORITY (Different from Track A)

Track B prioritizes narrative sources, not factual authority:

| Priority | Source Type | Why |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| 1 | First-person accounts | Highest narrative value |
| 2 | Forum threads with engagement | Real discourse |
| 3 | Long-form journalism profiles | Named individuals |
| 4 | Testimony transcripts | On-record statements |
| 5 | Documentary interviews | Personal perspective |

**De-prioritize:** News briefs, wire reports, official statements (Track A handles these)

---

## SEARCH EXECUTION

### Reddit/Forums

```
Search: [search_vector] site:reddit.com
Look for: High-upvote posts, personal stories, AMAs
Extract: Named individuals, specific claims, emotional content
```

### Testimony/Court Records

```
Search: [topic] testimony OR "testified" OR "statement"
Look for: Named witnesses, specific quotes
Extract: Full names, what they said, context
```

### Long-form Journalism

```
Search: [topic] profile OR investigation OR "spoke to"
Look for: Journalist interviews, subject profiles
Extract: Named sources, quotes, personal details
```

---

## FILTER CRITERIA

**INCLUDE:**
- First-person accounts with specifics
- Named individuals with actions
- Emotional content tied to facts
- High-engagement discussions

**EXCLUDE:**
- Speculation without sources
- Memes and jokes
- "I heard that..." claims
- Generic criticism without knowledge

---

## HARD CONSTRAINTS

- Search each role's vectors
- Prioritize engagement and first-person over authority
- Extract named individuals where found
- Do NOT include unsubstantiated speculation
- Do NOT include content unconnected to the topic

---

## COMPLETION RULE

Done when:
- All roles searched
- Each role has at least 2 sources examined
- Hot discourse identified (or marked as thin)
- Named individuals extracted where found
